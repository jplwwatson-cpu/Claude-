Put a real 3D scan of El Caballito here as `caballito.glb` and the Interactive mode uses it instead of the hand-built model.

How to get one: walk slowly around the statue on the Malecón with a free scanning app on your phone (Polycam, RealityScan or Scaniverse), then export as GLB (glTF binary). Keep the file under about 15 MB so the page stays fast.
